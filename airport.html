About the airport

Open to the public, kept by the District.

The Christmas Valley Airport is open to public use. For hangar space and tie-down arrangements, contact the District office at (541) 576-2216.

Please note that on-site fuel and FBO services are no longer available. Plan your fuel stops before you get here.
