A high-desert oasis

The valley’s gathering place on the water.

Baert Lake is looked after by the District and used for fishing, non-motorized boating and swimming from spring through autumn. In a cold winter, once the ice has been measured and found thick enough, it becomes the community’s skating rink.

It is a quiet, family spot. Bring a pole, a paddle, or just a picnic. There is no fee. We only ask that you pack out what you pack in, and leave it the way you would want to find it.
