An emerald in the high desert

Come as you are, play when you like.

The course sits in the heart of Christmas Valley, with long fairways, quick greens, and roughs that give way to native desert in a hurry. Golfers mix and match the tee boxes to make a full eighteen out of the nine.

This is a public course and no tee time is required. Larger groups planning a tournament are asked to call the office first so we can hold the date. Power carts are welcome on the fairways, with a wide berth around the greens and tee boxes.
