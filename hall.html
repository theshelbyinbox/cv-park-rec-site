The valley’s living room

A room that fits whatever you need it to.

The Community Hall is at 87345 Holly Lane and is available for anyone to rent. It suits groups large and small, from wedding receptions and family reunions to club meetings and holiday gatherings.

Call the District office at (541) 576-2216 for availability and to hold a date. A reservation is not confirmed until the rental agreement has been signed.
