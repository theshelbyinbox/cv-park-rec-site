HOW TO ADD REAL PHOTOS
======================
Drop a photo into this /images folder using the EXACT filename below, and it
will automatically appear on the site (replacing the illustration). Use .jpg
(or .png). Landscape photos look best. Suggested size ~1600x1000 px.

FILENAME              WHERE IT SHOWS
--------              --------------
hero-home.jpg         Home page — big top banner (wide landscape shot)
baert-lake.jpg        Home — Baert Lake card
golf.jpg              Home — Golf Course card
rodeo.jpg             Home — Rodeo Grounds card
community-hall.jpg    Home — Community Hall card
airport.jpg           Home — Airport card
field.jpg             Home — Multipurpose Field card
golf-hero.jpg         Golf page — big top banner (wide)
newspaper.jpg         Home — Desert Whispers feature image
location.jpg          Contact page — location/aerial image (optional)

Tip: until a photo is added, a hand-drawn illustration shows instead, so the
site always looks finished. Add photos whenever you're ready, one at a time.
