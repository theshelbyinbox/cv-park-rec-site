Where to go

Choose a property and you will find its rates, its hours and what is on site.

Each property has its own page with hours, fees and what you’ll find when you arrive. Anything the page doesn’t answer, the office will.

Free · Spring–Fall

Baert Lake

Fishing, non-motorized boating and swimming through the warm months, and the community’s ice rink when the ice runs thick.
Public · 9 holes

Golf Course

Long fairways and quick desert greens, built in 1964. No tee times needed.

Community Hall

About 2,100 square feet with a kitchen. Available to rent.

Rodeo Grounds & Arena

Horse shows, rodeos and clinics. Horses and camping are welcome here only.

Airport (62-S)

Public-use airstrip with a paved 5,200-foot runway.
Free · No reservation

Multipurpose Field & Playground

Baseball diamond, volleyball court and the children’s playground, just north of the library. Open to everyone, every day.
