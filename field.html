About the field

Free and open to everyone, with no reservation needed.

The multipurpose field sits just north of the library and is free and open to the public, the same as Baert Lake. It holds the community’s baseball diamond, a volleyball court and the children’s playground, and it gets used for practice, pickup games and long family afternoons.

Planning an organized tournament or a league and want the field to yourselves? Groups can reserve it for an event. Day-to-day use stays open to everyone.
