Publisher's Notice

What we will and won't print.

Freedom of the press gives the Christmas Valley Park & Recreation District control over what we publish in the Desert Whispers, including the right to refuse to print anything for any reason.

The District is not responsible for errors in articles or ads submitted to be published without change. Opinions expressed are not necessarily those of the Board, staff or advertisers.

Fair Housing

All real estate advertising in this newspaper is subject to the Fair Housing Act, which makes it illegal to advertise any preference, limitation or discrimination based on race, color, religion, sex, handicap, familial status, marital status or national origin. We will not accept real estate advertising that violates the law. All dwellings advertised here are available on an equal opportunity basis.
