Facilities & Properties

Six places that make this valley home.

Every one is open to the public and maintained by the District. Two are free to use any day of the year; four can be reserved for your event.

Free · Spring–Fall

Baert Lake

Fishing, non-motorized boating and swimming through the warm months — and the community's ice rink when the ice runs thick.
Public · 9 holes

Golf Course

Long fairways and quick desert greens, built in 1964. No tee times needed — $25 for nine.

Community Hall

About 2,100 sq ft with a kitchen. Available to rent.

Rodeo Grounds & Arena

Horse shows, rodeos and clinics. Horses and camping are welcome here only.

Airport (62-S)

Public-use airstrip with a paved 5,200-foot runway.
Free · No reservation

Multipurpose Field & Playground

Baseball diamond, volleyball court and the children's playground, just north of the library — open to everyone, every day.

The District

Established 1963, run by your neighbors.

A special taxing district in Lake County, Oregon. Five directors, elected by the district, preserve and operate the community's shared assets — and every meeting, agenda and set of minutes is public.
