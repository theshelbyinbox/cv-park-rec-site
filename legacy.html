Recreation · Open Spring–Fall

Baert Lake

The valley's gathering place on the water — fishing, paddling, swimming in summer, and skating in winter when the ice runs thick.

Open for the summer seasonLake is open for fishing, boating and swimming. Winter ice-skating status is posted here once the ice is measured safe — never go on the ice until the District posts the all-clear.

A high-desert oasis

Baert Lake is maintained by the Park & Recreation District and used for fishing, non-motorized boating, and swimming from spring through autumn. In a cold winter, when the ice is measured thick enough, it becomes the community's ice-skating rink.

It's a quiet, family-friendly spot — bring a pole, a paddle, or just a picnic. There's no fee to enjoy the lake; we simply ask that you pack out what you pack in and help us keep it beautiful for the next family.

Christmas Valley Golf Course

Long, narrow fairways and quick desert greens — once the longest nine in Oregon. No tee times needed.

An emerald in the high desert

Located in the heart of Christmas Valley, the course features long fairways, challenging greens, and roughs that quickly transition to native desert habitat. Golfers mix and match tee boxes for a fun, challenging 18-hole round.

We're a public nine-hole course — no tee times required. Large groups planning a tournament are asked to call the office to reserve. Power carts are welcome on the fairways; we just ask you give the greens and tee boxes a wide berth.

The District

Established 1963, run by your neighbors.

A special taxing district in Lake County, Oregon — preserving the community's shared assets for this generation and the next.

The Christmas Valley Park & Recreation District preserves and operates the community's shared assets — the golf course, Baert Lake, the rodeo grounds, community hall, airport, and ball fields.

Rodeo Grounds & Arena

An outdoor arena for horse shows, rodeos, clinics, and community gatherings — and the one spot where horses and camping are welcome.

Where the valley saddles up

The District's rodeo grounds host horse shows, rodeos, riding clinics, and large community events. Horses and camping are permitted only at the Rodeo Grounds — please keep both off the District's other properties.

Facilities · Public-Use Airstrip

Christmas Valley Airport (62-S)

A public-use airstrip maintained by the District, with a paved 5,200-foot runway serving the high desert.

Fly in to the valley

The Christmas Valley Airport (FAA identifier 62-S) is open to public use. For hangar space and tie-down arrangements, contact the District office at (541) 576-2216.

Please note: on-site fuel and FBO services are no longer available — plan your fuel stops accordingly.

Facilities · Open to All

Multipurpose Field & Playground

Just north of the library — a baseball diamond, a volleyball court, and the children's playground, open to everyone.

Room to play

The multipurpose field sits just north of the library and is free and open to the public — just like Baert Lake. It's home to the community's baseball diamond, a volleyball court, and the children's playground — a go-to spot for practice, pickup games, and family afternoons. No reservation needed; just come on out and play.

Planning an organized tournament or league and want the field to yourselves? Groups can reserve it for an event — but day-to-day use is always open to everyone.
